# Castrol AI Music Showcase

An internal, static showcase site that presents AI-generated music samples to the Castrol Vietnam marketing team so they can listen to and compare candidate songs for the mechanic-community campaign. It is a creative pitch, not the campaign platform.

## Language

**Showcase**:
This website — an internal/demo presentation of the AI music samples. Read-only, no accounts, no user content.
_Avoid_: App, platform, portal.

**Campaign Platform**:
The (separate, out-of-scope) public system where KOLs, the mechanic challenge, and community voting actually happen. This Showcase is explicitly NOT that.
_Avoid_: The site, the app.

**Campaign**:
Castrol Vietnam's marketing initiative themed around mechanics, repair, maintenance, customization, and automotive culture. Runs in four steps: AI songs → KOL music videos → mechanic video challenge → community vote for the winning hero.

**Song**:
One AI-generated music sample presented in the Showcase. Carries: title, genre/style, concept, short description, campaign fit, full Vietnamese lyrics, duration, and mood/tempo tags. Backed by one audio file.
_Avoid_: Track (fine casually), tune.

**Music Direction**:
The creative/stylistic lane a Song represents (e.g. rap-rock anthem vs acoustic V-pop). The Showcase's core value is letting stakeholders compare Directions.
_Avoid_: Style (use as a field label only), vibe.

**Campaign Fit**:
The per-Song explanation of how that Song could serve the Campaign — which moment or audience it suits.
_Avoid_: Use case, application.

**KOL**:
Key Opinion Leader / influencer who would turn a Song into a music video. Referenced only when explaining the Campaign; not a user of the Showcase.

**Hero**:
In Campaign terms, the winning mechanic contestant chosen by community vote. NOT to be confused with the visual "hero section" of a web page — always qualify which is meant.

**Mini-player**:
The persistent bottom bar showing the currently playing Song with seek/scrub and pause. Only one Song plays at a time; starting one pauses any other.
